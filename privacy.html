Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Who we are

"Should I Trust AI?" is a YouTube channel that tests free AI tools. This policy covers our website and "Should I Trust AI Bot", the private tool we use to manage our own channel.

What the tool accesses

The tool is used only by the channel owner, and only for the channel owner's YouTube account. With the owner's permission, given through Google's sign-in screen, it can:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and video details to our own channel, and
  • read our own channel's statistics (views, watch time, subscribers).

It doesn't access any other person's Google or YouTube account, and it doesn't collect any information about viewers beyond the anonymous totals YouTube Analytics provides to every channel owner.

How data is stored and shared

  • Sign-in tokens and channel statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer.
  • We don't sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use cookies, analytics or trackers here. GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and doing so deletes its sign-in tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/ShouldITrustAI/shoulditrustai.github.io.
